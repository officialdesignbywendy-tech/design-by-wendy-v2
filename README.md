# Wendy's Creative Studio

Create a modern, professional, and visually stunning portfolio website for Wendy, a Brand Identity, Marketing, and Promotional Designer. The website should have a creative agency feel rather than a generic graphic design portfolio. The overall aesthetic should be clean, premium, feminine, and strategic, with subtle pink accents, elegant typography, strong visual hierarchy, smooth animations, and a mobile-friendly responsive design.

Homepage

Create a powerful hero section with a professional headline, a short introduction, a call-to-action button, and a visually engaging design that immediately communicates creativity and professionalism.

About Me

Include an About Me section introducing Wendy as a passionate Brand Identity, Marketing, and Promotional Designer focused on helping businesses communicate effectively through strategic and visually compelling design.

Services

Create a services section featuring:

Brand Identity Design

Logo Design

Marketing Design

Promotional Design

Social Media Design

Flyer and Poster Design

Each service should have a brief professional description.

Portfolio

Create a visually appealing portfolio gallery showcasing projects in a clean grid layout. Include categories for:

Brand Identity Projects

Logo Design

Marketing Campaigns

Promotional Designs

Social Media Designs

Flyer and Poster Designs

Use professional mockups and project cards.

Case Studies

Include a dedicated case studies section where each project can present:

Project Overview

Client Goal

Design Process

Creative Solution

Final Outcome

The case studies should emphasize strategic thinking and problem-solving, not just visuals.

Why Work With Me

Instead of a testimonials section, create a "Why Work With Me" section highlighting:

Creative problem-solving

Attention to detail

Strategic design thinking

Consistency and professionalism

Strong communication

Passion for continuous learning

Also include a placeholder area where future client testimonials can be added.

Skills

Include a skills section featuring:

Brand Identity Design

Marketing Design

Promotional Design

Adobe Illustrator

Adobe InDesign

Canva

Typography

Layout Design

Visual Communication

Contact

Create a professional contact section with:

Contact form

Email placeholder

Social media links

Call-to-action encouraging potential clients to get in touch

Design Style

The website should:

Feel like a high-end creative studio

Use clean white space

Include subtle pink accents

Feature modern card layouts

Use smooth scrolling and animations

Have premium-looking project showcases

Be optimized for mobile and desktop

Be visually impressive enough to attract future clients and employers

Additional Requirements

Use placeholder images and portfolio projects that can be replaced later.

Write professional placeholder copy throughout the website.

Make the design feel trustworthy, polished, and modern.

Focus on showcasing both creativity and strategic thinking.

Ensure the website is easy to update as new projects and testimonials are added.
